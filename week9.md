# Chapter 9 — Eschatology and Non-Conformity: Lipscomb & the Civil War

[← All chapters](.)

**The Story:** Our movement's eschatology and non-institutional impulses led to bold non-conformity. That could look very different in different contexts.

**The Idea**: Our movement has the DNA to resist the tide.


**Framing Campbell on slavery (Micah, 2026-11-09):** he inherited enslaved people, eventually freed them, advocated gradual emancipation, and tried to hold the church's unity across even this question. It is the hardest test of "unity with freedom of opinion" (slaveholding treated as expediency, not a term of communion), and the war broke it.
