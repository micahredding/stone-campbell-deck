Every practice, three ways

One claim, run through everything this church does: each practice unites (no one fenced out), empowers (anyone may perform it), and anticipates (the age to come, arriving early). The rows are the practices. The columns are the class's three threads. Hover a row or column head to read it as one line of thought.

Congregation

Nothing central to capture

Autonomy as unity's odd guarantee: no headquarters to seize, no machinery whose control is worth a war. Union sought person by person, table by table.

W12W4 · Springfield
The congregation wields it

Plurality of elders; the minister under the elders; a presbytery writing its own last will and testament. Power fully distributed is still power.

W12 · Austintown 1830W4
A polity for the kingdom

Wild democracy as anticipation — allegiance already held for another government. The church governed as if the age to come were the real constitution.

W8 · Lipscomb

Unity, read down

The homecoming thread. Each practice refuses a fence: the test withdrawn, the creed dropped at the water, the one shared book, the audible mutual voice, the un-capturable center. What looks like absence is an opened door.

Priesthood, read down — governance

The middle column is the governance thread. Preside, baptize, interpret, sing, govern — five verbs, all in the hands of the congregation and the individual. Radical decentralization, but not a lack of power: the power didn't dissolve when the offices went; it moved into hands.

Future, read down

The engine. Every practice is the age to come, arriving early — a foretaste at the table, a promise at the water, a story read toward its ending, a song rehearsed, a polity anticipated. Take this column away and the other two stop making sense.

How this map connects

The causal spine, turned 90°

The chain — the table is the future → no fence → no gatekeepers → no caste → held by practices — is this grid read as a derivation. The chain proves the columns; the grid verifies them cell by cell.

The syllabus, as coverage

Weeks 1–8 narrate the Table row slowly — one row, told as story. Weeks 9–12 show the pattern repeating down the other rows. Week 0 could hand out this grid empty and let the class fill it in all semester.

Story → Idea → Future

The weekly template is one cell's journey: a scene (story), its column (idea), and the standing questions — what is beautiful here? what do we carry forward? (future).

The lineage — this grid against every prior collection of ideas (researched 29 Aug 2026)

The Show Bible's three principles (June)

The columns are a rediscovery: Unity in Christ · Priesthood of All Believers · Eschatology — the Show Bible's Part II, re-arrived-at from the practices side. It adds the center the grid is missing: the three are “three ways Christ alone becomes visible” — Christ / Spirit / Kingdom. Christ Alone is not a fourth column; it is what the columns are views of.

The minimalism map (May concept map)

A fourth-column candidate already written: open communion = fellowship minimalism, non-creedalism = doctrinal, a cappella = economic, governance = political, priesthood = interpretive. Five practices, five cells. The spine later re-derived minimalism as a consequence of the Priesthood column — column or corollary is an open call.

The homecoming register (spine)

Another candidate column: X as Homecoming — baptism-for-remission is Nicene, weekly communion is Justin Martyr, a cappella is the ancient East. Anchors exist for three of five rows. Where we are most distinctive, we are most catholic.

The binding-mechanism verbs (spine ruling)

The Priesthood column has an internal progression, not five parallel claims: baptism confers the standing, singing voices it, scripture equips it, governance exercises it — and the table is where it presides. Read in that order, the column is a life, not a list.

The interlock (Show Bible Part IV)

Three prior collections agree the columns are not equal: unity is eschatological, priesthood is eschatological. The Future column is not a peer — it is the floor the other two stand on. The chain is this same fact stated causally.

The bundle-concept warning (May essay)

The tradition is not a flat list but a network held in generative tension — unity the polar star, freedom the beating heart. A grid cannot show tension; it shows coverage. So the grid is one projection among several: the chain for narrative, the grid for verification, the naive/mature depth for discussion. None replaces the others.

A working object — rows, columns, and cells all revisable. Two cells stand open on purpose. Every prior map is honored, none is canonized.
