# Chapter 8 images

All four from Scott Harp, *History of the Restoration Movement*, "Hill Street Christian Church, Lexington, Kentucky":
https://therestorationmovement.com/_states/kentucky/hillstreet.htm (downloaded 2026-11-18).

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `cane-ridge-window-lexington-handshake.jpg` | Stained-glass window now standing in the Cane Ridge Meeting House, near Paris, Ky.: the handshake over an open Bible on the pulpit, the communion table (two plates, two cups) before them. Maker and date unknown (to find). |
| `cane-ridge-window-hill-street-meetinghouse.jpg` | Companion window at Cane Ridge: the Hill Street meetinghouse "where unity took place". |
| `bower-1955-hill-street-meetinghouse.jpg` | William Clayton Bower's 1955 painting of the Hill Street meetinghouse (as it looked when it served as a church). |
| `hill-street-meetinghouse-photo-1970s.jpg` | Framed photograph of the building in the 1970s, shortly before demolition (on display, apparently at Cane Ridge). |

Rights: photographs by the site's author; the windows and Bower's painting are 20th-century works. Fine for the class room; ask before putting them on the public site.
